Territory brands

A town, a brand, a crew. Steveston owns Richmond; West Vancouver owns the North Shore; Kitsilano owns the West Side; Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge each own their own town. Nobody drives into another brand's territory — the dispatch desk will not book it.

Specialty brands

Metro-wide, but a different job. New Garage Doors Vancouver installs new doors and openers only and hands every repair call to the local brand. YVR Garage Door Springs does one thing — springs — across sixteen cities. Hydraulic Hero serves industrial and commercial equipment everywhere, a market the door brands never touch.

Audience brands

Three metro-wide repair brands that speak to different customers: Probably Fine for the value-conscious homeowner, Good Enough for the straight-talk, honest-pricing buyer, and Sketchy for the person who has been burned before and wants the anti-scam promise. Different front doors, one calendar, no double-booking.

What flows between them

Referrals
Repair brands send new-door jobs to New Garage Doors; it sends repairs back to the territory brand; spring-only calls go to YVR.
One dispatch desk
Every lead is routed to exactly one company by postal code, service type and brand — territories are enforced in the system, not by goodwill.
Shared parts & fleet
One Richmond depot, one parts catalogue, one fleet and fuel program behind every truck.
One platform
Allegro X AI runs the websites, CRM, dispatch and phones for all twelve, so the Group learns from every call at once.

Technology

1 company

The Group's technology company: the shared software, data and applied-AI platform every operating business runs on.

Allegro X AI is the only company in the portfolio that does not answer a service call — and the reason every other company answers theirs on the same platform. It builds and runs the Group's shared technology, buys software and data once at group scale, and turns the operating data of twelve businesses into better systems.

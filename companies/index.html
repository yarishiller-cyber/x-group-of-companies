Technology

1 company

The Group's technology company: the shared software, data and applied-AI platform every operating business runs on.

Allegro X AI is the only company in the portfolio that does not answer a service call — and the reason every other company answers theirs on the same platform. It builds and runs the Group's shared technology, buys software and data once at group scale, and turns the operating data of twelve businesses into better systems.

Allegro X AI

Group technology company
Technology · Montréal, QC & Vancouver, BC

Allegro X AI builds and runs the shared technology platform behind every X Group company — websites, CRM, dispatch, telephony, AI call handling and analytics — and turns the operating data of twelve businesses into better systems. Headquartered in Montréal with an engineering team in Vancouver.

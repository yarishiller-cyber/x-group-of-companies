International

International opportunities

Our home market is Canada. As the Group grows, we evaluate selected international markets where our operating capabilities can be paired with skilled people, strong local partners and supportive business environments.

We approach international expansion deliberately. Before entering a market, we study its talent base, regulatory framework, operating costs, infrastructure, customer opportunity, technology ecosystem and long-term alignment with our businesses.

What we evaluate

Areas we study before we commit

Shared services & business operations

Finance, customer operations, technology, data and other functions supporting our portfolio.

Technology & AI

Engineering, applied AI, software and technology-enabled service delivery.

Operating partnerships

Joint ventures with qualified local operators.

Acquisitions

Established businesses where the Group can contribute operating expertise and long-term capital.

Specialized field services

Opportunities adjacent to our Canadian industrial and essential-service experience.

Markets under study

Central Asia & the Caucasus

One of several regions we are studying. We have not selected a destination, and this page deliberately does not rank markets. Russia and Belarus are excluded from our market-entry program; see our sanctions policy.

Uzbekistan

A national investment-promotion agency operating as a one-stop shop for foreign investors, and an IT Park actively courting international technology operations through soft-landing programmes.

Kazakhstan

A mature national investor agency (KAZAKH INVEST) offering full-cycle investor support, plus Astana Hub for qualified technology companies.

Kyrgyzstan

A presidential National Investment Agency with a single-window mechanism, alongside an export-oriented High Technology Park regime.

Azerbaijan

A single-window FDI-promotion agency focused on diversified, non-oil investment.

Georgia

An established FDI-promotion ecosystem and regional business positioning.

Armenia

A technology-talent angle under continued diligence.

We conduct this work as a Canadian company — engaging Canada's Trade Commissioner Service and following the Group's sanctions-screening and anti-bribery policies at every step.

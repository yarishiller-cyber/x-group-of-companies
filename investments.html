Five-step process

We move deliberately. Here is how we work with business owners.

  1. 1

    First call

    Confidential conversation about your business, your situation, and whether there is a fit. No forms, no NDAs unless you ask for them.

  2. 2

    Site visit

    We visit to meet your team, see your operation, and understand how you work. We look at systems, customer relationships, and your culture.

  3. 3

    Simple offer

    Based on what we learn, we make an offer. Not a term sheet filled with conditions — a real, straightforward number we will stand behind.

  4. 4

    30–60 day close

    Standard diligence, legal work and due diligence happen fast. We close on time. We do not renegotiate after an offer is made.

  5. 5

    Your name stays on the trucks

    Your brand keeps its name and identity. Your team stays. You have a path to stay involved or to step back, depending on what you want.

Our areas of interest

Where our capabilities give us an advantage

Essential services, industrial and field services, specialized B2B services, technology-enabled operations and adjacent categories where our existing platform provides an edge.

We do not describe ourselves as a private-equity firm, fund or institutional investor. We are an operator-led group that deploys its own capital and operating expertise. Metrics on this site are kept conservative and dated; we publish figures only once we can stand behind them.

  • Industrial & field services — mission-critical, mobile, recurring.

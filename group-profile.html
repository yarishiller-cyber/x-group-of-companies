Who we are

X Group Holdings Inc. (“X Group”) is a Canadian operating holding company based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. We build, acquire and support businesses across essential services, industrial field services and technology. Local brands, central operating infrastructure, disciplined capital allocation.

Operating model

Centralized group services — finance, procurement, people, technology, growth, operations and strategy — support lean operating brands that keep their own customers, teams and local identity.

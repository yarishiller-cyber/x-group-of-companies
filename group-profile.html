Who we are

X Group Holdings Inc. (“X Group”) is a Canadian operating holding company headquartered in Vancouver, British Columbia. We build, acquire and support businesses across technology, industrial field services and essential home services. Local brands, central operating infrastructure, disciplined capital allocation.

Operating model

Centralized group services — finance, procurement, people, technology, growth, operations and strategy — support lean operating companies that keep their own customers, teams and local identity. Allegro X AI, the Group's technology company, builds and runs the shared platform.

What we are

An operating group, not a brand exercise

A real group emerged because several operating businesses came to share owners, infrastructure, management, purchasing power, technology, dispatch, finance, recruiting and strategy. The parent exists to run that shared machinery well — and to decide where capital goes next.

  • We own and operate businesses.
  • We centralize the infrastructure that makes them better.
  • We reinvest capital and operating expertise into new businesses and opportunities.
  • We are Canadian by origin and selective about where we go next.

The people above work for the Group's companies every day: technicians, dispatchers, owner-operators and the founders who started the brands.

Technology inside the Group

Why a group of service businesses owns a technology company

A holding company of garage-door and hydraulic-service brands would normally rent a dozen software subscriptions, brand by brand, and never see the whole picture. X Group took a different route: it owns the team that builds and runs one platform for all of its companies.

One platform, not twelve subscriptions

Allegro X AI builds and operates the Group's websites, CRM, dispatch, telephony, AI-assisted call handling, payments and analytics. Every company runs on the same stack, so a lesson learned in Richmond is live in Maple Ridge the same week.

Buying power and brainpower

Software, telecom and data are bought once at group scale, and the engineers who build the platform sit inside the Group's operating cadence — a technology function no single brand could justify on its own.

A proving ground, then a product

Twelve live businesses give Allegro real dispatch, scheduling and customer data to learn from. Tools are tested on real trucks and real phone lines before they are offered to other trades businesses.

“The operating companies are Allegro's proving ground. Allegro is the Group's technology centre of gravity.”

What we believe

Principles we run the Group by

Operators, not spectators

We have built and run real service businesses. Every decision at the Group is made by people who have answered the phone, quoted the job and stood behind the work.

Local brands, central strength

Our companies keep their names, teams and communities. The Group carries the finance, technology, purchasing and systems that no single local brand should have to build alone.

Long-term ownership

We are not a fund with an exit clock. We buy and build businesses we intend to own for a long time, and we manage them that way.

Disciplined capital allocation

Cash generated by the portfolio is reinvested deliberately — into the businesses we have, the ones we acquire, and the markets we choose to enter.

Integrity that survives scrutiny

Honest pricing for customers, accurate books, real safety standards, and a public story that matches the private one. We design everything to reconcile under diligence.

People make the platform

Recruiting, training and a genuine safety culture are the product. The businesses are only as good as the crews and coordinators who run them.

Investment philosophy

We invest where operating expertise matters

We evaluate businesses and opportunities where disciplined operations, technology, centralized support and patient ownership can create long-term value.

Acquire

Established Canadian businesses where the existing team, customers and reputation deserve a long-term home.

Build

New operating businesses adjacent to our existing capabilities, launched on the Group's shared platform.

Partner

Joint ventures and strategic relationships with qualified operators.

Expand

Selected international markets where talent, demand and operating conditions create a compelling reason to establish a presence.

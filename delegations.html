International operations

Trade delegations

X Group hosts business delegations and issues IRCC-compliant letters of invitation on official letterhead for qualified foreign visitors to Canada.

We welcome structured visits from business delegations, technology partners, investment agencies, and international development organizations. Our letterhead meets Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) standards for letters of invitation supporting visitor visa applications.

Official letterhead

What our letters of invitation contain

Company registration & details

  • Registered legal name of X Group
  • Full Canadian business address
  • Company description & employee count
  • Year established & incorporation details

Visit details

  • Delegation purpose & objectives
  • Intended duration of visit
  • Cost responsibility & accommodation
  • Visitor names & organization

Authorized signatory

  • Officer name & title
  • Authority to issue invitations
  • Contact information for verification

Delivery & authenticity

  • Two signed originals on letterhead
  • IRCC-compliant format & content
  • Suitable for visa documentation

Typical visit agenda

  • Facility tours — Working visits to our operating companies, service facilities and technology operations in Greater Vancouver.
  • Technical presentations — Briefings on our operating model, service offerings, technology platforms and market focus.
  • Executive meetings — Discussions with Group leadership on strategy, partnerships, market entry and investment opportunities.
  • Memorandum of understanding — Negotiation & signature of preliminary collaboration or partnership terms where applicable.
  • Network & reception — Informal dinner or reception with business partners and relevant counterparts in the Vancouver market.

Gateway & logistics

Closest major international airport
Vancouver (YVR) — approximately 20 minutes by car from downtown Vancouver, and 30–40 minutes from Group facilities in Greater Vancouver.
